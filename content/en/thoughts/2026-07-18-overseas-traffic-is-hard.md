When you're building your own product, getting traffic really is one of the hardest parts. The anxiety comes in waves, and you're constantly looking for the next source of traffic.

Over the past few weeks, I had great reach on several Chinese social platforms. But I felt the conversion rate was only average, and some of the foundations inside my app weren't where I wanted them to be, so I deliberately slowed things down.

Over the last couple of days, I've started publishing content and looking for traffic again. Nothing I've posted in China has really taken off yet, but the results have been decent enough. What I want most right now is a breakthrough overseas—a viral post and a real surge of traffic. I've posted a few times on Threads and Instagram, but they've gotten virtually no traction. Anxiety is anxiety, but over the next few days I still need to keep experimenting, reading, and scrolling to learn what works.

Making a wish: I wake up and the numbers have exploded! Of course, that may just be a dream. In the end, I still have to keep my head down and do the work.
