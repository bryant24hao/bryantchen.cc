A few things I've learned from hands-on experience lately:

1. Putting the foundations in place first makes sense. It can save a lot of trouble later, but I need to balance that against the energy spent on overengineering.

2. When it comes to marketing and growth, I need to pay attention to content that's already a hit: imitate it, understand it, reach that level, then go beyond it. I can't just post whatever I feel like posting. Thinking about it, this is another way of approaching distribution with the user or audience in mind.

3. Keep up a steady, consistent marketing routine. Through volume and continuous experimentation, I can make up for what I lack in natural talent and an instinct for what works online.

4. A moderate amount of anxiety and a desire for positive feedback can drive frequent, effective action, which eventually brings good luck. I've seen this pattern play out several times on my indie development journey.

5. This week, it feels like the account ban has disrupted my marketing and work rhythm. I need to avoid letting external factors interrupt my routine work like this again. I need a backup, both in my mindset and in my actual toolkit. I can't leave it to chance or passively wait for things outside my control to change.
