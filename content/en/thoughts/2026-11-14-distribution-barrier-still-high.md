The barrier to making a product has dropped, but the barrier to distribution hasn't, at least not anytime soon.

Building a high-quality demo is cheaper than ever. But getting a product onto the App Store, distributing it well, and reaching users at scale has barely gotten any easier. If anything, it's harder than before.

In early October I spent three days building a new product. App review then took three weeks, and a small beta plus fixing the issues testers raised took more than another week. Tonight I went out and tested it myself again. While using it, once I caught myself thinking "this is actually pretty good," I knew it was time to ship.

Just now I submitted it for review again. The new version should be out soon, and then it's on to promotion and distribution.
