I didn't write a weekly post last week or this week. I've been too busy, and there wasn't anything I particularly felt like writing about. Tonight, just before bed, the urge to write happened to come along, so here are a few thoughts.

## Minimalist entrepreneurship

For a while, I didn't know what to call what I'm doing. Both "one-person company" and "indie development" get used so loosely now that they've even picked up some negative associations. Recently, while rereading *The Minimalist Entrepreneur* (published in Chinese as *小而美*), the phrase "minimalist entrepreneurship" really stayed with me. It seems to fit both my current state of mind and the way I'd ideally like to work: build something I enjoy, want to keep putting time into, and can earn a healthy, sustained profit from. Of course, if you ask whether I've figured it all out, I'd say you can't really think your way to a complete answer. I'll probably keep feeling my way forward and understand it more deeply through doing.

## Holding on until you get over the hill

I've had this feeling several times lately, especially over the past couple of days. Looking back, it feels like holding my breath through a difficult stretch. Before I make it through, the pressure keeps building, and I often find myself working away while doubting what I'm doing. But once I hang on through that stretch and get a little positive feedback "by luck," I finally see what the view looks like from the other side of the hill. It would have been such a shame if I hadn't held on.

## Placing a bold order with the universe

Before going to sleep yesterday, I quietly made a wish: I hoped I'd wake up to a surge in the product's ranking and see it in the top 10. When I woke up and opened my phone, there it was, at No. 7 in one market, with YouTube behind it. Friends, have dreams and ambition. Make bold wishes to the universe!

Yes, when you've been working steadily, at the right pace and with a sound approach, believe in yourself and place that order boldly. The universe will receive it.

## Celebrate often, and success will follow

I said something similar in an earlier weekly post. Here it is again.

I used to think "celebrate often, and success will follow" sounded like cheesy success advice, a bit over the top. Lately, though, I've found there really is something to it. If you don't like celebrating your wins, it means you don't love or want it enough. People who celebrate often and do the work in a practical, methodical way tend to have luck on their side.

And with that, good night.
