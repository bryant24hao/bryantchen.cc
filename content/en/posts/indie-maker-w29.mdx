## 0810: On work

Monday afternoons usually aren't my most productive, so I spent some time reading instead. I came across four lines from Toni Morrison about work, and they really landed.

The four lines come from Toni Morrison's [*The Work You Do, the Person You Are*](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2017/06/05/toni-morrison-the-work-you-do-the-person-you-are). I made them into a poster.

1. Whatever the work is, do it well — not for the boss but for yourself.
2. You make the job; it doesn't make you.
3. Your real life is with your family.
4. You are not the work you do; you are the person you are.

![A poster of Toni Morrison's four lines about work](/images/posts/indie-maker-w29-morrison-poster.jpg)

I don't work for a company at the moment, but I'm still creating, working, and building products — and some of the principles above are always worth sitting with:

- Work for yourself, and don't let the work alienate you from who you are.
- Good work shapes a better version of you.

## 0814: On marketing the product

I've put real effort into posting and replying these past couple of days, and yet the offhand replies seem to work better. Why? I need to look into it more, but one guess: when you're trying hard to share from a selling posture, even genuine effort carries a whiff of self-interest.

The posts I reply to offhand are mostly written when I have downtime, or when I'm out somewhere — and chances are the people on the other side of that post are in the same state. On top of that, when I reply from that place, it comes from real enthusiasm and honest sharing.

A few moments that moved me this week, all from users:

The first was a note of encouragement: "your passion for making software comes through the screen — I'm sure your app will be a success." See? As long as you express and share honestly, users can feel it.

![Encouragement from a user in my DMs](/images/posts/indie-maker-w29-user-dm.png)

The other one also came from an overseas user, who even typed out "jiayou" in pinyin for me. That was touching — and it's exactly what makes continuous iteration worth it.

![An overseas user writing "jiā yóu" in pinyin](/images/posts/indie-maker-w29-threads-reply.png)

## 0815: Apple's Small Business Program finally came through

After more than a month of waiting — three emails to support along the way, plus a phone call yesterday — my Small Business Program application was finally approved.

![The Small Business Program welcome email from Apple](/images/posts/indie-maker-w29-sbp-email.png)

What's the upside? Previously every user subscription charge had 30% taken off the top; from here on it drops to 15%. Effectively a 15% raise without doing anything.

So, another reminder to anyone planning to build an iOS app: apply for the Small Business Program the moment you launch!

## 0816: A few product milestones

Today the product's official Xiaohongshu account passed 1k followers. That account is only two months old.

The other thing — half funny, half not — is that the product is now being ripped off. Friends who follow along know my product is currently Apple-only; I haven't had the bandwidth to ship on Android. But today I got a message from a user: "I downloaded this product on xx platform, and it doesn't seem like the one in your video." I took a look: it borrows the ColorWander name and its logo is a malicious AI-mangled version of mine. Beyond that, it has nothing to do with my product.

If you're a ColorWander user, please keep your eyes open: the only official version right now is on iOS. Android is in the works, and I'll announce it properly when it ships. Please don't support pirated copies.
